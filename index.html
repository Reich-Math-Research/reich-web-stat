Wall-Sun-Sun Primes in Generalized Fibonacci Sequences

An investigation into anomalous divisibility properties of second-order Lucas recurrence sequences. The analytical framework employs Yu's theorem on p-adic linear forms in logarithms to establish explicit bounds on how divisible Lucas sequences can be.

Advanced Theoretical Draft

Zero-Density Asymptotics of Exceptional n-ABC Tuples

An advanced draft bounding the asymptotic density of rare arithmetic exceptions within generalized n-dimensional formulations of the ABC Conjecture. The proof constructs a geometric structure with combinatorial methods to count a highly structured arithmetic set, applies p-adic measure theory to compute the probability of explicit arithmetic collisions on this set, and uses real analysis to complete the proof of zero density.

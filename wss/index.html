Analytic Number Theory

Wall-Sun-Sun Primes in Generalized Fibonacci Sequences

A comprehensive five-month analytical study theoretically bounding arithmetic anomalies in second-order recurrence relations.

This research examines extreme variations of Wall-Sun-Sun primes generalized through higher divisibilities across varying initial parameters of Lucas sequences. By introducing modular congruences that help p-adic logarithmic bounding techniques the project derives a universal bound that applies to every Lucas sequence in the same way.

Research Architecture and Technical Overview

Objective

Isolate and bound the divisibility conditions under which anomalous prime divisors manifest in recurrence structures.

Methodology

Application of algebraic modular arithmetic and transcendental number theory techniques, specifically linear forms in p-adic logarithms.

Conclusion

Formulation of a verified mathematical theorem suitable for academic presentation and peer-review.

The priority of this monograph is absolute logical validation, in favor of rigorous, step-by-step arithmetic and analytic verification.

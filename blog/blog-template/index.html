Refining Bounds via p-adic Linear Forms in Logarithms

Date: 2026-07-03 • 12 min read • Verification: Programmatic

An investigation into explicit lower bounds for linear forms in p-adic logarithms yields crucial constraints on the growth rates of exceptional sequences.

Methodological Overview

By applying Yu's fundamental theorems on p-adic logarithmic forms, we can bound the valuation of generic terms without relying on heavy heuristic assumptions. This forms a foundational building block for bounding periodic loops within generalized Fibonacci sequences.

Key Formulation Matrix: Let \(\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_n\) be algebraic numbers inside an algebraic number field of degree \(d\). We map the bounding constraints across structural spaces to limit exceptional behavior anomalies.

Algorithmic Extensions

To evaluate these constraints under practical limitations, auxiliary sieve logic routines were developed to compute explicit structural deviations up to \(10^{14}\). The bounding inequalities systematically hold true against all evaluated primes.
